Government Schemes for Farmers

Karnataka & Central Govt - 100% verified links

1. PM Kisan Samman Nidhi ⭐ Popular

💰 Benefit: Rs.6000/year directly to bank account in 3 installments

👨‍🌾 Eligibility: All small & marginal farmers with land

💸
Apply Now → pmkisan.gov.in

2. Raitha Nidhi - Karnataka

💰 Benefit: Additional Rs.4000 from State Govt (Total Rs.10000)

📍 Only for Karnataka farmers - Apply via Raitamitra

🌾
Apply Now → raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in

3. PM Fasal Bima Yojana - Crop Insurance

🛡️ Benefit: 50% subsidy on insurance premium, full claim if crop fails

🌧️ Covers: Flood, drought, pest attack

🛡️
Apply Now → pmfby.gov.in

🚰 For: SC/ST & Small farmers - Free borewell + pump set

📍 Apply at: Taluk Social Welfare Office
